# DJMemory Launch Communications

Last updated: August 13, 2026.

## Community announcement

DJMemory is a new Mac app built to protect the sets DJs already record. When a completed recording appears, DJMemory preserves a copy, keeps it organized, and leaves the original file untouched.

The first private beta is focused on real Serato and rekordbox workflows. Your audio stays on your Mac by default, and local protection does not depend on an account.

I am inviting a small group of DJs to test setup, reliability, and trust before opening access more widely. If you record your sets and want to help shape it, join the waitlist.

## Onboarding email

**Subject:** Protect your first set with DJMemory

DJMemory works best when it has access to the folder where your DJ software saves completed recordings.

Open Protection, choose your Serato or rekordbox recording folder, and check that the status reads Protected. DJMemory copies completed recordings into its archive; it does not move, rename, or delete the source.

After your first protected set, we’ll ask three short questions about setup, confidence, and trust.

## First-set follow-up

**Subject:** Did DJMemory protect the set the way you expected?

You protected your first set. We’d like to know whether the result was clear:

- Could you find the protected recording?
- Did you understand what happened to the source file?
- How confident are you that DJMemory will work next time, from 1–5?
- What felt confusing or unnecessary?

## Thirty-day follow-up

**Subject:** Has DJMemory become part of your set routine?

Over the last month, did DJMemory protect more than one real set? Tell us what kept it useful, what made you turn it off, and what would make it worth paying for after beta.

## Tester safety note

DJMemory is beta software. Keep your existing recording and backup routine while testing. DJMemory creates protected copies but is not a replacement for a separate-device backup. Audio and full tracklists stay local unless you explicitly export or share them.
