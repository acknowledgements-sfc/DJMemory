# DJMemory Beta Research and Launch Playbook

Last updated: August 13, 2026.

## Cohort plan

| Phase | Size | Recruitment | Objective | Exit gate |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder circle | 5–8 | Direct outreach to trusted DJs | Installation, setup, and trust problems | No source-file risk; useful diagnostics; setup blockers documented |
| Invite beta | 25–40 | Community waitlist, balanced by workflow | Repeated protection and message fit | At least 60% activation and 40% second-set protection within 30 days |
| Open beta | Controlled public access | Landing page, referrals, community partners | Broader compatibility and acquisition | Clean installation, support capacity, and stable critical workflows |

## Interview sample

Recruit 8–12 DJs across club, mobile/event, radio/streaming, touring, and hobbyist contexts. Prioritize behavioral variety over demographic quotas.

Capture:

- DJ software and macOS version
- performance and recording frequency
- current recording and archive workflow
- last time a recording was lost, forgotten, or hard to find
- library/cloud tools already used
- trust concerns about folder access and background apps
- response to the three benefit territories
- perceived value and pricing thresholds

## 30-minute interview guide

1. Tell me about the last set you recorded from beginning to end.
2. What happened to the file after you stopped recording?
3. Show me where recordings normally land, if you are comfortable.
4. When was the last time you could not find or use a set you expected to have?
5. What do you currently back up: tracks, DJ library data, finished sets, or all three?
6. What would make you distrust an app watching a recording folder?
7. React to each benefit concept: valuable, believable, and different?
8. What would you need to see before installing this before a real gig?
9. After seeing the product, ask pricing-sensitivity questions.
10. What would make you keep it running for the next month?

## Survey

Use the enriched waitlist fields for lightweight qualification, followed by a five-minute survey for invited testers:

- Have you ever lost or misplaced a completed DJ recording?
- How many sets did you record in the last 30 days?
- How many can you find in under one minute?
- Rank protection, organization, compatibility, publishing, and cloud backup.
- Rate trust in DJMemory from 1–5 after setup and after the first archive.
- How disappointed would you be if DJMemory were no longer available?

## Campaign

### Objective

Recruit 25–40 qualified US Mac DJs for invite-only beta and generate enough real usage to evaluate second-set retention within 30 days.

### Core message

Your set should not disappear into a folder you forget. DJMemory quietly protects the completed recording, keeps it findable, and leaves the original untouched.

### Channels

1. Direct messages to trusted DJs—highest context and feedback quality.
2. Rob's existing DJ community—community post plus personal follow-up.
3. Email waitlist—cohort invitations and learning updates.
4. Partner referrals from promoters, radio hosts, and DJ educators after the founder circle.
5. Public social and relevant communities only when clean installation is ready.

Do not use paid acquisition during invite beta. The constraint is learning and support quality, not reach.

## Funnel and events

Track: `page_view`, `demo_view`, `waitlist_started`, `waitlist_joined`, `research_completed`, `invite_sent`, `invite_accepted`, `setup_completed`, `first_set_protected`, and `second_set_protected`.

Report weekly by source, DJ type, software, and cohort. Never attach track titles, artists, audio paths, or audio content to marketing events.
