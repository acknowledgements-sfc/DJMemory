# DJMemory Brand Strategy

Last updated: August 13, 2026.

## 01 — What is DJMemory?

> **Every set deserves a memory.**

DJ sets are living work: practiced, improvised, shaped by a room, and gone when the night ends. DJMemory was built on the belief that preserving that work should not depend on remembering one more task at load-out.

DJMemory is a local-first Mac app that protects completed recordings and turns them into a usable archive. It works quietly, shows its work clearly, and respects the boundary between helping an artist and taking control of their files.

## 02 — Vision

To become the trusted memory layer for recorded DJ performance—built on artist control, dependable automation, and open compatibility.

**Trusted · Local · Open · Enduring**

## 03 — Mission

Make it easy for every DJ to preserve, understand, and return to their recorded work. DJMemory protects the finished set, keeps useful context attached, and never makes cloud custody the price of safety.

**Protect · Organize · Respect · Remember**

## 04 — Principles

**Artist control.** The DJ decides where recordings live and what leaves the computer. Local operation is a product boundary, not a slogan.

**Proof over promises.** Show the protected file, archive time, source status, and support level. Trust comes from visible evidence.

**Quiet reliability.** The product should reduce post-set work rather than create a new management ritual.

**Honest compatibility.** Supported, partial, and manual workflows must be labeled plainly. Ambiguity is more damaging than a smaller compatibility list.

**The work matters.** A set is not disposable content. It is part of an artist's development and history whether or not it is published.

## 05 — Brand goals

1. Establish “automatic set protection” as a recognizable product category.
2. Earn trust with 25–40 invite-only testers before opening the beta.
3. Prove repeated value through a second protected set within 30 days.
4. Build independent DJMemory recognition, separate from Beat Revival.
5. Create a language and visual system that can extend from Mac utility to artist archive.

## 06 — Personality

**Serious 6/8 · Casual 3/8 · Accessible 3/8 · Innovative 6/8 · Muted 3/8**

DJMemory feels like the calm person in the booth who checks the recorder, labels the cable, and does not interrupt the set. It is technically fluent without performing expertise. It respects the emotional value of the work but does not romanticize routine product behavior.

## 07 — Voice

The brand speaks with **calm precision**—clear, protective, respectful, and grounded.

- **Plainspoken:** Use short sentences and familiar verbs. Explain what happened and what the DJ can do next.
- **Specific:** Say “DJMemory copied the completed recording” instead of “your memory is safe.”
- **Reassuring:** Reduce uncertainty with evidence, not generic comfort language.
- **Artist-respecting:** Treat recordings and track histories as private creative work.
- **Quietly confident:** State verified capability directly; do not shout or posture.
- **Honest:** Name limitations, permissions, and manual steps before they surprise someone.

### Say this / not this

| Say this | Not this |
| --- | --- |
| Your completed recording was copied to DJMemory. | Your set is safe forever. |
| The original file was not changed. | We secure all your music. |
| Serato and rekordbox are supported first. | Works everywhere. |
| Your audio stays on this Mac by default. | Military-grade privacy. |
| DJMemory needs access to this recording folder. | One-click magic. |
| Join the private beta. We are testing with real DJ workflows. | The future of DJing is here. |

## 08 — Messaging territories

### Lead territory: Protection

- **Headline:** Your set ended. Its story shouldn't.
- **Support:** DJMemory automatically protects completed recordings on your Mac and leaves the originals untouched.
- **Tagline:** Every set, remembered.

### Supporting territory: History

- **Headline:** Build a record of the work.
- **Support:** Keep the recording, date, venue, notes, and track history connected.

### Supporting territory: Compatibility

- **Headline:** One archive habit across DJ apps.
- **Support:** Start with Serato and rekordbox, with honest support paths for the rest of your setup.

### Alternate taglines

1. **Every set, remembered.** Best balance of emotional value and product truth.
2. **Protection for the work you played.** More functional and ownable.
3. **Keep the set. Keep the story.** Strong for campaign language.
4. **Your sets. Still yours.** Strong privacy/control territory.
5. **The memory layer for DJs.** Strong category language, less immediate for a homepage.

## 09 — Visual direction

- **Mood:** After-hours calm, not nightlife spectacle. The room after the peak moment, when the work is being kept.
- **Palette:** Near-black ink, warm bone, muted violet, and a restrained signal green for protected states.
- **Typography:** A modern grotesk for clarity paired with a mono face for timestamps, file evidence, and support states.
- **Imagery:** Real booths, laptops, hands, recording folders, and post-set moments. Avoid anonymous festival crowds, neon waveforms, helmets, and generic AI gradients.
- **Interface motif:** A horizontal “memory line” connecting Recording → Protected → Remembered.
- **Motion:** Slow status transitions and precise progress. No pulsing alarms unless action is genuinely required.
- **Accessibility:** High contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and status communication that does not rely on color.

## 10 — Brand architecture and domain

DJMemory is the product brand. Beat Revival should not appear in customer-facing headings, metadata, emails, or sign-up confirmation. Until a dedicated domain is selected, the existing infrastructure may host the page, but public links should move to a DJMemory-owned domain before open beta.

Domain criteria: short, spoken clearly in a booth, no hyphen, no confusion with memory utilities, and capable of supporting app, help, and account subdomains. Verify availability and trademark risk before purchase; do not imply availability from an unverified lookup.
