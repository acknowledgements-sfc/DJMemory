# DJMemory Market and Competitor Deep Dive

Last updated: August 13, 2026.

## Executive read

DJMemory should not enter the market as another DJ library manager, cloud locker, or production suite. The clearest opportunity is a narrower category: **automatic set protection**.

The category promise is simple: after a DJ finishes recording, DJMemory preserves a copy, keeps it organized, and leaves the original untouched. That job is underserved across Serato, rekordbox, Traktor, VirtualDJ, and djay Pro.

## Market gaps

1. **The finished set is not the library.** Competitors protect source tracks or help create mixes. DJMemory can focus on the unique artifact created during performance.
2. **Protection without custody.** Cloud competitors ask DJs to upload or sync. DJMemory can prove safety while the artist keeps control of the audio.
3. **One layer above DJ software.** Native record buttons are tied to a single app and require memory. DJMemory can provide a consistent protection layer across workflows.
4. **Archive context without publishing pressure.** Event, venue, date, notes, and track history can make a set findable without forcing public sharing.
5. **Trust through visible restraint.** Copy-only behavior, local-first defaults, and explicit support labels are differentiators when demonstrated concretely.

## Ranked opportunities

1. Own the phrase and category **automatic set protection**.
2. Make “source recordings are never moved, renamed, or deleted” the strongest proof point.
3. Recruit DJs who record at least monthly and have already lost, misplaced, or failed to label a recording.
4. Lead with Serato and rekordbox proof while showing an honest compatibility roadmap.
5. Build a visible protection status and archive receipt after every successful save.
6. Add optional publish/export workflows only after protection retention is established.

## Threats and watch list

- Serato or rekordbox could add automatic post-recording organization and backup.
- Stereo Aura could expand beyond Serato and become the default automatic recorder.
- Library products could add watched recording folders as a small feature.
- Mac-only distribution reduces the reachable market and raises clean-install expectations.
- A failed archive or unclear permission state would damage the trust position disproportionately.
